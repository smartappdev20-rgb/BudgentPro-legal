100% local. No financial data is sent to our servers. File exports stay on your phone. Only Google AdMob collects the advertising ID, and only to display and measure ads.

Budgent Pro is a personal budget tracking application. This policy explains what data is processed, why it is processed, how long it is kept, and how you can exercise your rights.

0. A 100% local budget

Budgent Pro runs entirely on your phone. Your budgets, transactions, goals, categories and receipts never leave the device. There is no Budgent Pro account, no cloud synchronization and no hidden upload.

  • File exports (JSON, CSV, PDF and encrypted backup) stay on your phone and are shared only with the application you choose.
  • Only Google AdMob collects the advertising ID in order to display ads and measure their performance.
  • Optional anonymous statistics contain no financial data and are disabled by default.

1. Who we are

Budgent Pro is published by the developer identified on the app's Google Play listing. The app has no backend server for storing your budget data and does not ask you to create an online account.

The official contact channel is the support section of the Budgent Pro Google Play listing or the in-app support form (Settings > Support). You can also write to smartappdev20@gmail.com.

2. Data stored on your device

The data you enter is stored locally in an encrypted SQLite database on your phone or tablet. It is not transmitted to our servers.

  • transactions, amounts, dates, labels and notes;
  • budgets, categories, limits and savings goals;
  • recurring transactions and category splits;
  • receipt photos you choose to attach;
  • app preferences (language, currency, theme and security).

This data remains on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.

3. Optional encrypted local backup

Backups are strictly local. You can export an encrypted snapshot to a location of your choice (device storage, a file space or another destination you select). No cloud provider is connected to the app and no backup is sent automatically.

The snapshot is encrypted on your device with AES-256-CBC and authenticated with HMAC-SHA256. The recovery key stays on your device. Without that key, the backup cannot be decrypted, including by us. You are responsible for the exported file, for keeping it and for deleting it.

4. Google AdMob advertising

Budgent Pro is free and funded by advertising. The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google and its advertising partners may collect or receive technical data such as the advertising ID, IP address, device information, ad interaction data and diagnostics.

These data are used to display ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. They do not include the content of your transactions, budgets or categories. Depending on your consent and applicable law, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Google advertising policy: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Optional anonymous statistics

Anonymous usage measurement can be enabled with your explicit consent. It is disabled by default. It records only usage events (for example, opening a screen or completing onboarding) and contains no amount, transaction label, category or banking identifier.

You can disable this option at any time in Settings > Privacy.

6. No collection on a third-party server

Budgent Pro does not operate a backend server to collect or host your financial data. No budget data is sold, rented or transmitted to a data broker. The only third-party services used are:

  • Google AdMob for advertising;
  • the optional anonymous measurement service, only if you enable it;
  • the support endpoint, only when you voluntarily send a request (subject, message, app version, language and platform).

7. Retention

  • Local data: kept until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • Local backup: the exported file is kept until you delete it; temporary internal copies are erased with the app data.
  • Support: a support message is processed by the publisher for as long as needed to answer, then deleted according to its retention policy.
  • Advertising: duration defined by Google under its own policies.
  • Anonymous statistics: duration defined by the measurement service, without financial data.

8. Security

Budgent Pro relies on your operating system sandbox and offers a PIN code and/or biometrics to protect access to the app. Local backups are encrypted with AES-256-CBC and authenticated with HMAC-SHA256 before being written. No system is infallible: protect your device, your PIN and your recovery key.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, port, restrict or object to the processing of your data, as well as the right to withdraw your consent.

You can exercise these rights directly in the app:

  • view and edit your data in the Budget and Transactions screens;
  • export your transactions as CSV from the settings;
  • delete all your data with the "Delete my account and data" button;
  • disable anonymous statistics in the settings.

10. Complaints

For any question about your data, use the in-app support form (Settings > Support) or write to smartappdev20@gmail.com. You may also lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority.

11. Children

Budgent Pro is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data about children. If you believe that a child has used the app, delete the data from the settings or uninstall the app.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the app or in applicable law. The last update date is shown at the top of this document. In the event of a material change, information will be displayed in the app or on the Google Play listing.

13. Contact

The official contact channel is the in-app support form (Settings > Support) or smartappdev20@gmail.com. Never share your recovery key, your PIN or your email credentials with anyone.
